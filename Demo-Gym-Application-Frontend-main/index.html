Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about how GymFlow operates? We have answers.

Is this frontend prototype connected to a real live database?
No. This is a pure frontend demonstration architecture. All changes, new members, registrations, and payment entries persist in your browser's LocalStorage using a structured mock database layer (GymFlowDB). No real banking or servers are contacted.
How do I switch between Super Admin, Gym Admin, and Member roles?
You can click the one-click demo login buttons on the Login Page, or use the floating "Switch Demo Role" widget at the bottom right corner of any dashboard page.
Can I export reports and invoices to CSV or print them?
Yes! All member tables, transaction tables, and reports support 1-click CSV download, and invoices support clean, browser-native print preview dialogs with mock tax and currency calculations.
Is the design responsive for tablets and mobile devices?
Absolutely. The entire navigation collapses into a mobile slide-out drawer, tables scroll smoothly horizontally with sticky identifiers, and cards adapt cleanly across all screen sizes.
