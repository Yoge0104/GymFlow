Exercise Coaching Notes

Barbell Bench Press

  • Retract and depress scapulae to protect rotator cuff tendons.
  • Lower the bar with controlled cadence to lower sternum (nipple line).
  • Maintain 5 points of contact: head, upper back, glutes, and both feet flat on floor.
  • Drive through heels and push elbows inward without flaring past 75 degrees.

If experiencing any joint irritation, notify Coach David Miller to substitute with neutral grip dumbbell presses.
